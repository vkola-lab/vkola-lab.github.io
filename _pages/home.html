Boston University · AI & human health

Multimodal AI for neurodegenerative disease and human health

We are computer scientists, neuroscientists, engineers and physicians building methods that fit the science, not science that fits the methods. Our models diagnose dementia, estimate disease biomarkers and interpret medical images, and we release them openly.
