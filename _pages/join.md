## Current and admitted students

If you are a **current student**, or **already admitted** to an academic or medical degree program at **Boston University** or a nearby academic center, we would be happy to discuss rotation openings.

Please contact one of our **postdoctoral scholars** or **graduate students** listed on the [Team page](/team/) to set up an appointment at one of our [two locations](/contact/).

## Prospective applicants

We receive many inquiries and have limited capacity to work with students who are **not yet admitted** to a graduate or professional program. We can't offer research opportunities, mentorship or individual guidance while you are applying, and contacting the lab will not affect admissions decisions, because the lab has no formal role in program admissions.

> Because of the volume of inquiries, we only reply to applicants who are a strong match for our current research needs. If you don't hear from us, we wish you the best in your academic and research pursuits.
